03 — Foundations

Spacing

The source declares no spacing variables; these steps are the values the skill and the showcase actually use, named on the Tailwind stems they appear as. Radius is one token, rarely used. Fixed sizes and opacities complete the set.

Layout

Content is centered at content-max (1100px; 900px inside the sidebar layout) with space-6 gutters. Sections take space-16 above and below with a 1px top border. Cards pad space-6; the showcase gallery pads space-5. Buttons pad space-2 by space-4. Inputs pad space-2 by space-3.
