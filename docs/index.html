Lux Swiss

Two colors, one accent, no shadows

Lux Solari's house design language — Swiss-minimalist, duotone-strict. Two colors, one strong accent; everything else is weight, space, and contrast. This site is the showcase and the documentation: every value on it is the system's own, and every demo is built from its tokens.

Two rules govern everything

01 — Duotone strict

foreground and background are the two functional colors; primary, Blood Red, is the lone accent. No success green, no info blue, no second accent. Win/loss, active/inactive, emphasis and error are all weight, size, spacing and contrast. If you feel the urge to add a color, add weight 700, a size step, or whitespace instead.

02 — Swiss-minimalist

Borders are 1px solid border, full ink in light and full cream in dark. No shadows: elevation is the one background step from background to card. Whitespace is generous. Labels are uppercase mono with 0.2em tracking. Corners are square; radius exists but is rarely used, and rounded-full is for 6px status dots only.

“Difference is expressed through weight, space, and contrast — never by adding a color.”

Using the system

  1. 01Copy skills/lux-swiss/assets/theme.css into your global stylesheet. It defines every token for light and dark, both font flavors and the Jost accent, wired to Tailwind 4 via @theme inline; on other stacks the :root / .dark / .geist / .jost blocks work as plain custom properties.
  2. 02Pick a font flavor, Space (default) or Geist, and load only that flavor's two families plus Zilla Slab from Google Fonts. Add Jost 700 only if the heading accent is on.
  3. 03Build with the semantic token, never a raw hex: bg-background, text-foreground, border-border, bg-primary. Compose from the component patterns; every demo on this site is plain HTML on these tokens and can be copied.

Dark mode is the .dark class on <html>, persisted in localStorage under theme. The Geist flavor is .geist; the Jost heading accent is .jost. All three compose independently — try the toggles in the sidebar.

House Mark

How Lux Swiss and its sibling Tri-Swiss share governance and differ in palette.

Do not

  • No success green, info blue or second accent. Weight, size or layout instead.
  • No shadows. Depth is border presence and one background step.
  • No rounded-full on containers or pills. Dots only.
  • No raw hex in markup. Always the semantic token.
  • No chart libraries except restyled Observable Plot.
  • No icons but restyled Lucide. No emoji in UI text.
  • No accent-colored list markers or table borders.
  • No full-color images outside the photography-content exception.
  • The Structural Block and the brand-moment device are not new colors: a layout job for the one accent, and a typographic device.
